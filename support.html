Frequently Asked Questions

What types of NFC tags work with FrigMate?

FrigMate supports standard NDEF-compatible NFC tags including NTAG213, NTAG215, NTAG216 (ISO 14443 Type A), ISO 15693 tags, FeliCa, and MIFARE tags. Standard waterproof NTAG213/215 stickers work great on refrigerator and freezer containers.

Can I use FrigMate without physical NFC tags?

Yes! When checking in an item, simply tap Check In Without NFC Tag (Virtual Tag). You can manage, edit, and check out virtual-tag items directly from the Inventory list, and choose from over 55 food icons if you don't take a photo.

How does Smart Scan work?

Open the Scan tab and tap Scan NFC Tag. If the tag's stored text matches an item currently in your inventory, FrigMate automatically opens the Check Out / Edit sheet. If the tag is empty or new, FrigMate opens the Check In form so you can add a new item right away.

What happens to the NFC tag when I check out or edit an item?

When you partially check out or edit an item linked to a physical NFC tag, FrigMate prompts you to scan the tag and updates the remaining quantity directly on the tag. When you check out the full quantity, FrigMate automatically erases the NDEF text on the tag so the sticker can be reused on any new food container.

How does photo recognition and item counting work?

During Check In, tap Take Photo or Photo Library. FrigMate runs Apple's on-device Vision framework (image classification, OCR text reading, and saliency/contour object detection) to suggest the food's name, estimate the item count, and recommend a storage location and expiration date.

How do I use the AI Meal Planner & AI Shopping Assistant?

Tap the AI Chef button on the Inventory tab (or AI Shopping List Recommendations on the Shopping tab). FrigMate formats your current refrigerator inventory—highlighting items expiring soon—into a structured prompt that you can send in one tap to ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, or any app via the iOS Share Sheet.

How do I customize expiration notifications?

Tap the gear icon in the top-left corner of the Inventory tab to open Notification Settings. You can toggle expiration reminders on or off and choose how many days in advance (1 to 14 days) you want to be notified before items expire.
