Designed for an Effortless, Waste-Free Kitchen

📲

Smart NFC Scan & Auto-Routing

Hold your iPhone near any NFC tag. If the tag matches an item in your fridge, FrigMate opens Check Out / Edit. If the tag is empty or new, it opens Check In. Full check-outs automatically erase the tag for reuse.

📸

Vision Food Recognition & Counting

Snap a photo to automatically recognize produce, dairy, meat, and packaged groceries using on-device Vision and OCR—complete with automatic visual object counting and storage zone suggestions.

🍽️

AI Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner Planner

Share your live fridge inventory to ChatGPT, Claude, or Google Gemini in one tap. FrigMate prioritizes ingredients expiring soon so you can cook delicious meals before food goes bad.

🛒

Smart Shopping List & 1-Tap Restock

Items are automatically added to your Shopping List when checked out completely. Use the AI Shopping Assistant to suggest groceries that pair with what's already in your fridge.

🥚

55+ Custom Food Icons & Virtual Tags

Don't have physical NFC stickers or a photo handy? Use Virtual Tags and pick from over 55 food icons (Eggs, Milk, Cheese, Produce, Meat, Seafood, Bakery, and more).

🔔

Smart Expiration Alerts & 100% Private

Separate default shelf life for Cooler (14 days) and Freezer (30 days) with customizable advance notifications. All photos and inventory stay locally on your iPhone.
